Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about caring for your leather goods and order fulfillment.

Avoid submerging in water. If dry, apply a light coat of natural beeswax or mink oil balm once every 6 months. Wipe clean with a soft dry microfiber cloth.
Initially, our slim cardholders fit 4-6 plastic cards plus folded cash notes. Full-grain leather naturally molds to your carry over the first 2 weeks, allowing up to 8 cards comfortably without losing form.
We stand by every stitch. If your wallet experience unraveling saddle stitching, edge splitting, or structural failure under normal use, we will repair or replace your item free of charge for life.
Standard un-stamped goods have a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Bespoke pieces customized with your initials are final sale, but covered under our full lifetime repair guarantee.
